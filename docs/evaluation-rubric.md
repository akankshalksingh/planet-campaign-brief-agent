# Evaluation Rubric

The evaluator scores every brief from 1 to 5 across four dimensions.

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Relevance | Does the Planet use case match the company's actual industry and needs? |
| Specificity | Is the brief clearly about this company, not generic? |
| Groundedness | Are claims supported by company context or retrieved Planet content? |
| Actionability | Can a marketer or sales rep use the recommendation? |

The app flags a brief when total score is below 14, any individual score is 2 or lower, live context is weak, or the vertical requires extra human review.
